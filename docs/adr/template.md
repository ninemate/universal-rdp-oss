# NNNN. Title in the imperative ("Use X for Y")

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Deprecated | Superseded by NNNN (link the superseding ADR)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who made or must make the decision
- **Related:** links to other ADRs, research notes, issues

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints (technical, legal, people, time), and the facts the decision rests on. Cite sources, usually a section of `docs/research/`. Mark anything you could not check as **Unverified:** with the reason.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly and testably ("We will…"). Include scope: what the decision covers and what it deliberately leaves open.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. Include new risks, follow-up work, and how we would notice that the decision was wrong (a trigger for revisiting it).

## Alternatives considered

Each option we did not choose, with the reason in a line or two.
